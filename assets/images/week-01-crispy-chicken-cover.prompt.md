# 第 1 周封面图生成提示词

生成方式：Codex 内置图像生成工具。

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape recipe cover and plating reference for a personal Western-cooking learning web app
Primary request: create a refined but achievable bistro-style plated meal for a beginner lesson: pan-seared boneless skin-on chicken thigh with very crisp deep-golden skin, plain creamy mashed potatoes, buttered asparagus, bright green broccoli, caramelized brown mushrooms, and only a small amount of light chicken pan jus
Scene/backdrop: one warm light-gray ceramic round plate on a quiet neutral stone tabletop, no extra dishes or props competing with the food
Subject: chicken is the clear visual focus; chicken and mashed potatoes are separate and do not overlap; 3 to 4 asparagus spears create a directional connection; broccoli and mushrooms are clustered rather than evenly scattered; mushroom browned faces visible; pan jus appears as a few restrained dots and a short curve beside the chicken, never poured over the crisp skin
Style/medium: high-end natural restaurant food photography, realistic home-cook-achievable portions and textures, not over-styled, no impossible garnish
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, slightly overhead three-quarter camera angle, full plate visible, generous negative space on the plate, asymmetric fine-dining composition, no cropped plate edges
Lighting/mood: soft window light from upper left, warm restrained bistro mood, accurate food colors, crisp texture detail
Color palette: golden brown chicken, cream potato, fresh green vegetables, warm brown mushrooms, neutral plate and background
Constraints: exactly one plated serving; no carrots; no chicken on top of mashed potatoes; no sauce on the chicken skin; no excessive microgreens; no utensils; no hands; no text; no logo; no watermark
Avoid: stacked tower presentation, circular ring of vegetables, huge sauce pool, raw-looking chicken, burned skin, fake plastic food, extreme shallow focus, black plate
```
