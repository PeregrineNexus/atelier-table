# 第 1 周摆盘变化图生成记录

生成方式：Codex 内置图像生成工具。三张图片均为 3:2 横图，用于菜谱详情页的摆盘灵感区。

## 切片扇形

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape recipe plating inspiration for a Western-cooking learning web app
Primary request: create an achievable refined fine-dining plating variation of pan-seared boneless skin-on chicken thigh, plain creamy mashed potatoes, buttered asparagus, bright green broccoli, caramelized brown mushrooms, and a very small amount of light chicken pan jus
Scene/backdrop: one warm white round ceramic plate on a quiet neutral stone tabletop
Subject: slice the rested crisp chicken thigh into four thick pieces and fan them slightly at the upper-right, keeping the golden crisp skin visible on every slice; place one neat oval quenelle of mashed potato at lower-left, separate from the chicken; place three asparagus spears diagonally between them; cluster two small broccoli florets and three browned mushroom halves near the potato; add a short restrained crescent of pan jus beside and partly under the chicken, never over the skin
Style/medium: realistic high-end restaurant food photography, achievable by a careful home cook, natural food textures
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, slightly overhead three-quarter angle, full plate visible, compact asymmetric triangular composition with at least half the plate left as clean negative space
Lighting/mood: soft natural window light, warm restrained bistro mood, crisp texture detail
Constraints: exactly one plated serving; use only the listed ingredients; chicken and potato must not overlap; no carrots; no sauce on chicken skin; no excessive garnish; no text; no logo; no watermark
Avoid: stacked tower, circular ring of vegetables, huge sauce pool, raw-looking chicken, burned skin, plastic food, black plate
```

```text
Subject: keep the crisp chicken thigh whole and place it at the left-center, skin facing up; form a short smooth horizontal swipe of mashed potato at the right-center, clearly separate from the chicken; align three asparagus spears through the middle as a clean diagonal bridge without crossing over the chicken; cluster broccoli tightly at one end of the potato swipe and browned mushroom halves at the other; place three small pan-jus dots and one short line underneath the visual axis, never touching the crisp skin
Style/medium: realistic contemporary bistro food photography, elegant but reproducible at home, natural proportions and textures

Constraints: exactly one plated serving; use only listed ingredients; no carrots; no chicken on mashed potato; no sauce on the skin; no microgreen pile; no utensils; no text; no logo; no watermark

```

## 自然新月弧线

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape recipe plating inspiration for a Western-cooking learning web app
Primary request: create an organic crescent plating variation of pan-seared boneless skin-on chicken thigh, plain creamy mashed potatoes, buttered asparagus, bright green broccoli, caramelized brown mushrooms, and a very small amount of light chicken pan jus
Scene/backdrop: one pale gray round handmade ceramic plate on a subtle neutral linen-covered table
Subject: place the whole crisp chicken thigh near the upper-center, skin fully visible; place a soft spoon-shaped mound of mashed potato at lower-left, separate from the chicken; angle three asparagus spears from lower-left toward upper-right to begin a loose crescent; continue that curve with two broccoli florets and a tight cluster of caramelized mushroom halves, leaving irregular natural spacing; finish the arc with five tiny graduated pan-jus dots beside the chicken, never on its skin
Style/medium: realistic elegant naturalistic restaurant food photography, artistic but achievable for a beginner, honest ingredient texture
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, 45-degree overhead three-quarter view, full plate visible, flowing crescent composition with a clear focal point and about forty percent negative space
Lighting/mood: gentle daylight with subtle warm shadows, refined relaxed bistro atmosphere
Constraints: exactly one serving; only listed ingredients; no carrots; chicken and mashed potato separate; sauce does not touch crisp skin; no herbs or flowers added; no text; no logo; no watermark
Avoid: perfect circular ring, rigid symmetry, stacked tower, large sauce pool, overcrowded plate, raw-looking chicken, burned skin, artificial food
```
